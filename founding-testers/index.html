Meet VerbCompass

Built for people who are genuinely learning Spanish.

VerbCompass combines a detailed Spanish verb reference with focused study tools designed to help learners understand, organize, and practice the verbs they actually need.

We are not looking for professional software testers. We are looking for Spanish learners who can use the app naturally and tell us what works, what feels confusing, and what could be made better.

Inside the App

See what you will be helping test.

VerbCompass includes powerful Reference tools, complete conjugation information, active-recall Study sessions, CEFR-based learning controls, and a personalized Custom Verb Pool.

Why VerbCompass Exists

Created by someone who wanted a better way to study Spanish verbs.

VerbCompass began with a simple frustration: while studying Spanish, I found myself constantly jumping between textbooks, websites, conjugation charts, and notes just to understand a single verb.

I wanted one place that combined a complete verb reference with practical study tools that let me focus on the verbs I actually needed to learn.

After months of development and thousands of carefully organized Spanish verbs, VerbCompass has grown into the study companion I wished I had when I started learning.

VerbCompass includes over 2,600 carefully organized Spanish verbs, complete conjugations, CEFR organization, customizable study sessions, and a powerful personal verb collection.

Now I'd like a small group of fellow learners to help make it even better before its public release.

Why We’re Testing

Real learners notice what developers cannot.

The app’s core Spanish reference and study structure is already built. Before VerbCompass becomes publicly available, we need to learn how reliably and clearly it performs during real Spanish study on different Android devices.

Your experience can help us improve navigation, explanations, study behavior, device compatibility, and the overall learning experience.

Those lessons will strengthen the first Spanish release and help establish the foundation for VerbCompass’s next intended direction: English verb support for English learners, beginning with Spanish speakers.

How It Works

A simple, personal testing process

The program is designed to fit naturally into your existing Spanish study rather than feel like a technical testing assignment.

  1. 1

    Send a short request

    Tell us your Spanish level, how you currently study, and the Google-account email you will use with Google Play.

  2. 2

    We review it personally

    We’ll review your request personally. If your request is a good fit for the Founding Tester program, we’ll add your Google-account email to the Closed Test.

  3. 3

    Receive your welcome email

    If we add you to the Closed Test, we’ll email you the Google Play tester link and brief instructions for getting started.

  4. 4

    Become a tester and install VerbCompass

    Open the tester link while signed in to the same Google account, click Become a tester, and install VerbCompass on your Android device.

  5. 5

    Use it during the 14-day testing period

    Remain opted in, keep the app installed, and use it naturally during your Spanish study throughout the required period.

  6. 6

    Share honest feedback

    Use the Founding Tester Feedback form to tell us what worked well, what felt confusing, and what would make VerbCompass more useful to you. You can send feedback more than once during the test.

Frequently Asked Questions

Before you submit your request

Who should become a Founding Tester?

The program is intended for people actively learning, teaching, or regularly using Spanish who have an Android device and a Google account. Founding Testers must be age 18 or older.

Do I need advanced Spanish?

No. Feedback from beginners, intermediate learners, and advanced learners can all help improve VerbCompass.

Do I need technical testing experience?

No. We want you to use VerbCompass as a language learner and describe your experience in your own words.

Why do you need my Google-account email address?

Google Play controls Closed Test access through a tester list. We need the exact Google-account email you will use with Google Play on your Android device so we can add it to that list.

What happens after I submit my request?

We’ll review your request personally. Submitting the form does not immediately add you to the test. If your request is a good fit for the Founding Tester program and we add you to the Closed Test, we’ll email you everything you need to get started, including the Google Play tester link and brief instructions.

What device and account do I need?

The current test requires an Android phone or tablet and a Google account. The account signed in to Google Play on that device must match the email address you submit here.

Why can’t I join directly from this page?

We must first add your Google-account email to the Google Play Console tester list. After that, we can email you the Google Play tester link that lets you opt in and install VerbCompass.

How long will testing take?

Google Play requires testers to remain opted in for at least 14 consecutive days. Remain opted in, keep the app installed, and use VerbCompass naturally during your normal Spanish study throughout that period.

How do I share feedback?

Use the Founding Tester Feedback form. Tell us what worked well, what felt confusing, and what would make the app more useful for your Spanish study. You may submit the form whenever you have something useful to share.

When will I receive Lifetime Full Access?

Active Founding Testers who remain opted in through the required testing period, use the app, and share feedback will receive Lifetime Full Access to VerbCompass — Spanish Verb Companion on Android after the public launch. This includes future updates and upgrades to that app and will be associated with the Google account used during testing.
