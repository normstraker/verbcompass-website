Meet VerbCompass

Built for people who are genuinely learning Spanish.

VerbCompass combines a detailed Spanish verb reference with focused study tools designed to help learners understand, organize, and practice the verbs they actually need.

We are not looking for professional software testers. We are looking for Spanish learners who can use the app naturally and tell us what works, what feels confusing, and what could be made better.

Inside the App

See what you will be helping test.

VerbCompass includes powerful Reference tools, complete conjugation information, active-recall Study sessions, CEFR-based learning controls, and a personalized Custom Verb Pool.

Why VerbCompass Exists

Created by someone who wanted a better way to study Spanish verbs.

VerbCompass began with a simple frustration: while studying Spanish, I found myself constantly jumping between textbooks, websites, conjugation charts, and notes just to understand a single verb.

I wanted one place that combined a complete verb reference with practical study tools that let me focus on the verbs I actually needed to learn.

After months of development and thousands of carefully organized Spanish verbs, VerbCompass has grown into the study companion I wished I had when I started learning.

VerbCompass includes over 2,600 carefully organized Spanish verbs, complete conjugations, CEFR organization, customizable study sessions, and a powerful personal verb collection.

Now I'd like a small group of fellow learners to help make it even better before its public release.

How It Works

A simple testing process

The program is designed to fit naturally into your existing Spanish study rather than feel like a technical testing assignment.

  1. 1

    Request an invitation

    Email us with a short note about your Spanish learning experience and the Android device you plan to use.

  2. 2

    Join the closed test

    Approved testers will receive instructions for joining the VerbCompass closed test through Google Play.

  3. 3

    Install VerbCompass

    Download the test release on your Android phone or tablet using the Google account included in the test.

  4. 4

    Use it while studying

    Explore Reference, build a Custom Verb Pool, and complete Study sessions as part of your normal Spanish learning.

  5. 5

    Share honest feedback

    Tell us what worked well, what felt confusing, and what would make VerbCompass more useful to you.

  6. 6

    Receive Lifetime Full Access

    Testers who complete the program will receive permanent Full Access as our thank-you for helping prepare VerbCompass for launch.

Frequently Asked Questions

Before you apply

Who should become a Founding Tester?

The program is intended for people actively learning, teaching, or regularly using Spanish who have access to an Android device.

Do I need advanced Spanish?

No. Feedback from beginners, intermediate learners, and advanced learners can all help improve VerbCompass.

Do I need technical testing experience?

No. We want you to use VerbCompass as a language learner and describe your experience in your own words.

How long will testing take?

Testers will be asked to keep the app installed and use it during the Google Play closed-testing period. Additional instructions will be included with the invitation.

How will my email address be used?

Your email address will be used only to administer the Founding Tester program, provide testing instructions, and communicate about your tester benefits.

VerbCompass itself does not require a VerbCompass account or login.
