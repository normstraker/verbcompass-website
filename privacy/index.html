Privacy Policy

Your learning data belongs to you.

VerbCompass is designed to collect as little information as possible and to keep your study data on your own device.

Effective Date: September 1, 2026

Definitions

For the purposes of this Privacy Policy:

  • VerbCompass refers to the VerbCompass application, its official website, and related services.
  • You refers to the individual using VerbCompass.
  • We, our, and us refer to the developers and publishers of VerbCompass.

Welcome

Thank you for using VerbCompass.

VerbCompass is designed to help you learn Spanish while respecting your privacy. We believe your learning data belongs to you. Most information created while using the app remains on your own device, and we collect as little information as possible.

This Privacy Policy explains what information VerbCompass uses, how it is stored, and the limited situations in which third-party services may process information on our behalf.

Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to:

  • The VerbCompass mobile application
  • The official VerbCompass website
  • Related services provided by VerbCompass

By using VerbCompass, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

The VerbCompass application does not require you to create a VerbCompass account or sign in. Study information created in the application is stored locally on your device as described below.

If you voluntarily submit the Founding Tester Request form on our website, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Your Google-account email address
  • Your Spanish level
  • The ways you currently study Spanish
  • An optional message you choose to provide

We use this information only to review requests, administer Google Play Closed Testing, communicate with testers, collect feedback, and administer Founding Tester benefits.

If you voluntarily submit the Founding Tester Feedback form, we may collect your Google-account email address, feedback category, description, optional device model and Android version, and your optional permission for us to contact you about the report.

We use feedback submissions to identify problems, understand the testing experience, improve VerbCompass, follow up when permitted, and document the results of the Closed Test.

Do not use the Founding Tester Request form to submit passwords, financial information, phone numbers, home addresses, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Your study activity remains on your own device unless you choose to share it, such as when sending us feedback.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

VerbCompass stores certain information locally on your device to provide application functionality.

This information may include:

  • Application settings
  • Study preferences
  • Custom Verb Pool selections
  • Study progress
  • Practice history
  • Other application data required to provide normal application functionality

This information is stored only on your device and is not transmitted to VerbCompass servers.

3. Purchases

If you purchase the Full Version of VerbCompass, payment processing is handled entirely by the Google Play Store, or by the Apple App Store on future iOS releases.

VerbCompass does not receive or store your payment information, including:

  • Credit card numbers
  • Debit card numbers
  • Bank account information
  • Payment credentials

Purchase verification is performed through the official billing services provided by the applicable app store.

4. Third-Party Services

VerbCompass relies on certain third-party platform services to provide application functionality.

Current third-party services may include:

  • Google Play for application distribution and billing
  • Google Play Billing for Android purchases
  • Google Play Console for administering the Closed Testing tester list
  • Netlify for processing website form submissions
  • Apple App Store and StoreKit for future iOS distribution and purchases

If we add you to the Closed Test, your submitted Google-account email address may be added to the Google Play Console tester list so that Google Play can grant you access to the test release. Netlify processes and stores information submitted through the Founding Tester Request and Founding Tester Feedback forms on our behalf.

Our email service provider may process your email address and correspondence when we communicate with you about the Founding Tester program.

These services operate under their own privacy policies and may collect information according to their respective practices.

If additional third-party services are introduced in future versions of VerbCompass, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

5. Advertising

VerbCompass does not display third-party advertising.

We do not use advertising identifiers for marketing purposes.

6. Analytics

At the time of this policy, VerbCompass does not use analytics services to collect information about how you use the application.

7. Children's Privacy

VerbCompass is an educational application intended for language learning. The Founding Tester program and its request form are for people age 18 or older.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If our privacy practices change in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to reflect those changes.

8. Data Security

Your learning data remains on your own device.

While no software can guarantee absolute security, we minimize the collection of personal information and follow platform best practices when implementing application features.

9. Founding Tester Information Retention and Requests

Information from requests that do not result in participation in the Founding Tester program may be retained until no later than 90 days after the Founding Tester program ends.

For participating Founding Testers, we may retain limited contact and participation information as needed to administer testing and provide promised Founding Tester benefits, including Lifetime Full Access to VerbCompass — Spanish Verb Companion on Android and its future updates and upgrades, associated with the Google account used during testing.

Founding Tester feedback may be retained as needed to improve the app, document the Closed Test, and maintain a record of issues and changes. We may remove identifying contact information when it is no longer needed for those purposes.

To request correction or deletion of information submitted through a Founding Tester form, email privacy@verbcompass.com.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically as VerbCompass evolves.

When significant changes are made, the Effective Date at the beginning of this document will be updated to indicate the most recent revision.

Our Privacy Philosophy

VerbCompass exists to help people learn languages—not to collect personal information.

We believe educational software should respect user privacy by default, store data locally whenever practical, and collect only the information necessary to provide the features users expect.

That philosophy guides the design of VerbCompass today and will continue to guide future development.
