What comes next

A foundation for English learners

The first public release will support English-speaking learners of Spanish. What we learn from the web edition and Android test will also help establish VerbCompass’s next intended direction: English verb support for English learners, beginning with Spanish speakers.

That future experience will be designed around the particular challenges English verbs present rather than simply translating the current Spanish app.
