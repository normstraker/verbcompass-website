# Founding Tester reward terms — proposed October 22, 2026

Status: concrete proposal for owner review; not approved for publication.
These terms support recruiting for the existing EUV closed test now. Finishing
SUV or the complete public purchasing system is not a prerequisite to accepting
testers. Do not confuse current unrestricted use with permanent Play ownership.

## Offer

Full Spanish Android access during testing, at no charge. After qualification,
permanent Full Access to the Spanish course and its updates in the same Android
app, associated with the Google account used during testing. No recurring fee.
No promise of separately sold future courses/products. Honor broader benefits
previously promised individually.

## Participation

- Age 18 or older, compatible Android device with Google Play, correct account.
- Apply, receive personal invitation, opt in and install from Google Play.
- Stay opted in for at least 14 consecutive days starting at Play opt-in, not
  application date. Use Reference and Study in normal Spanish-learning sessions
  during that period; no invented Google requirement for daily use or hours.
- Submit at least one honest private feedback report. Problems, confusion and
  suggestions qualify; no positive-feedback or public rating/review condition.
- After the participation period, notify us through the existing feedback form
  that testing is complete. Include the same Google-account email and opt-in
  date. Do not send passwords, payment details or purchase tokens.

## Proposed timetable — explicit owner decision needed

1. Review reported completion and email confirmation or a specific explanation
   of any missing requirement within **7 calendar days** of that report.
2. Provide earned permanent access before future paid restrictions affect
   qualifying testers, at no charge. Preserve current full testing access while
   implementing and verifying the permanent grant. No arbitrary 30-day deadline.
3. A public-release delay does not cancel earned benefits. Confirm a workable
   grant and restoration route before publishing; do not promise an unverified
   fallback. Seven-day review is an operational target pending final terms.
